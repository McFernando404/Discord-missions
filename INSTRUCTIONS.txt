===================================================================================================
                                  QUEST COMPLETER - INSTRUCTIONS
===================================================================================================

ENGLISH
-------

> 1. Download Discord Canary from: https://canary.discord.com/download
> 2. Close Discord Canary completely
> 3. Open this file in Notepad as Administrator:
     %AppData%\discordcanary\settings.json
> 4. Add this line inside the JSON:
     "DANGEROUS_ENABLE_DEVTOOLS_ONLY_ENABLE_IF_YOU_KNOW_WHAT_YOURE_DOING": true
> 5. Save the file and reopen Discord Canary
> 6. Go to Discover > Quests and accept any quest you want to complete
> 7. Press Ctrl + Shift + I to open Developer Tools
> 8. Go to the Console tab
> 9. Type: allow pasting
> 10. Press Enter
> 11. Open the file code.js and copy ALL the content
> 12. Paste it into the console
> 13. Press Enter
> 14. The quest panel will appear in the bottom right corner

Notes:
> The panel is draggable (move it by the header)
> Click Eject to stop everything
> Click X to close the panel
> Some quests require manual completion (shown with a lock icon)

-------

> 1. Descarga Discord Canary desde: https://canary.discord.com/download

     %AppData%\discordcanary\settings.json

     "DANGEROUS_ENABLE_DEVTOOLS_ONLY_ENABLE_IF_YOU_KNOW_WHAT_YOURE_DOING": true
> 5. Guarda el archivo y vuelve a abrir Discord Canary
> 6. Ve a Descubrir > Quests y acepta la quest que quieras completar
> 7. Presiona Ctrl + Shift + I para abrir las Herramientas de Desarrollador
> 8. Ve a la pestana Console
> 9. Escribe: allow pasting
> 10. Presiona Enter
> 11. Abre el archivo code.js y copia TODO el contenido
> 12. Pegalo en la consola
> 13. Presiona Enter
> 14. El panel de quests aparecera en la esquina inferior derecha

Notas:
> El panel se puede mover (arrastralo desde el titulo)
> Click en Eject para detener todo
> Click en X para cerrar el panel
